# Glid

**Self-custody bitcoin with the calm of a modern money app.**

Glid is an open-source Bitcoin wallet for iPhone, for people who earn and hold bitcoin and want it to feel as easy as the rest of their money. Your keys stay on your phone. Nobody else holds your money, and nobody else can move it.

> Status: in development. Not yet available.

---

## Why Glid exists

Most wallets are cold and technical, crowded with coins you never asked for, or built for experts. Meanwhile, modern money apps have changed what people expect: organised, pleasant to open, written in plain language, and theirs to personalise. Bitcoin wallets haven't caught up.

As a result, many people keep their bitcoin on exchanges because holding it themselves feels risky, overwhelming and confusing. Those who do hold it are rarely sure they've set it up right.

## What we believe

**People look after what they feel they own.** Glid is designed to feel like *yours*: your wallets, your colours, your names. Money you feel attached to is money you protect.

**Your first self-custody wallet should feel calm.** The first move off an exchange and the first backup are the moments that matter most. Glid makes the safe way to back up the easy way.

**Speak human.** Glid shows your own currency first. There's no jargon and no hype, just plain English.

**Security at the core.** Everything in Glid is built on keeping your bitcoin safe. Protecting your keys comes before every feature, design choice and convenience.

## Glid for personal use

- **Multiple wallets.** Keep separate wallets for savings, spending, a gift or your business, so you always know where your money is and what it's for.
- **Themes.** Give each wallet its own personality with colours and visual styles.
- **Contacts and labels.** Save the people you pay and label every transaction, so your history still makes sense months later.
- **Value at the time.** Every transaction shows what it was worth in your currency on the day it happened.
- **Export.** Download your history as a CSV for your records or your accountant.
- **Payment requests.** Ask for an amount with a QR code or a link. A fresh address each time keeps your history private from the people who pay you.
- **Calm by design.** Bitcoin's price moves a lot, and watching it constantly leads to anxiety and rash decisions. Glid has no price-watching dashboard, just one clear view of how your holdings have changed, so you can hold with confidence.

## Glid for business

*Planned. An optional subscription for businesses.*

Bitcoin for business is even less well served. More businesses transact in bitcoin, yet wallets offer them nothing beyond sending and receiving. The alternatives are services that hold your funds for you, or accounting platforms built for large finance teams. Most businesses end up pasting addresses into invoices and rebuilding their records by hand.

The records are the hard part. Income is valued on the day it arrives, spending bitcoin can create a gain or loss, transfers between your own wallets aren't income at all, and the balance moves with the price. It isn't a standard profit and loss, and the tax rules vary by country.

Glid for business takes care of that work, in plain business language and without giving up custody:

- **Accounting automation.** Every transaction categorised and valued, with gains and summaries ready for your accountant, or synced to tools such as Xero and QuickBooks.
- **Invoices with payment pages.** Send clients a link they can pay from, and get notified the moment it's paid.
- **Team access.** A partner or accountant can view the business wallet, and payments can require approval.
- **Later:** shared wallets that need more than one person to approve a payment (multisig), and a Lightning address like `studio@glid.cash`.
- **And all the convenience of Glid for personal use.** Multiple wallets, themes, contacts and labels, value at the time, and the same calm design, all included.

## Open source

Glid's code will be public. Anyone can read it, audit it and check how it handles keys.

- **Free and open:** the whole app, including every feature that runs on your phone.
- **Paid:** the business subscription, for services that run on Glid's servers. You pay for a service someone operates, not for locked-away code.
